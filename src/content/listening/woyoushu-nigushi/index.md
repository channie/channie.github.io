聽珍妮的podcast，我是帶著崇拜的眼神，眼睛冒著星星。

愛閱讀、讀很多書、侃侃而談、溫柔的聲線、清楚的咬字、喜歡冰美式，都是我嚮往的。
但不是我，也不是我喜歡的。

像一條我很欣賞、很喜歡、很美很美的長裙，
不適合我的身型，
但每次經過，還是會忍不住多看幾眼。


I first found Jenny's podcast through her Hygge episode. It has so many of the things I aspire to: a warm voice, crisp pronunciation, notes and depth from the book(s), and stories from her travels and her roommate. Her work has become a strong inspiration for, and influence on, my own podcast.
