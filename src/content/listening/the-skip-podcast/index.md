I started following Nikhyl's work when The Skip was still just a blog, sharing nuggets of wisdom, career frameworks, and guidance for people facing decisions in the tech industry. His content is aimed at product managers, but I find much of it just as relevant to engineering leaders.

Nikhyl has a fascinating way of giving advice with real substance, delivered in a no-bullshit way. It is neither one-size-fits-all nor, more importantly, another unhelpful "it depends," which is all too often what you hear when you feel lost and go looking for career advice.
