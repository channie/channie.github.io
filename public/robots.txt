# Keeping a page out of search is done with a `noindex` robots meta plus
# sitemap exclusion (see src/data/seo.ts) — deliberately NOT with a Disallow
# here. Disallow blocks crawling, and a page that is never crawled is never
# read, so its noindex meta would never be seen. The two would cancel out.
User-agent: *
Allow: /

Sitemap: https://channie.org/sitemap-index.xml
